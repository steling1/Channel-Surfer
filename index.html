Survive eight classic-inspired games in one continuous run. Each sudden switch gives you a brief control cue and a safe moment to get your bearings.

Use arrows / WASD to move and Space to jump, shoot, or flap. Snake changes direction with arrows. Breakout follows left and right. Racing uses all four directions.

Shooter, runner, Breakout, racing, flight, and Asteroids switch after you survive the timer. Snake and Frogger have no time limit: finish the displayed target without taking a hit to switch. A hit costs a life and resets that channel’s target progress; your total score is kept. Waiting in objective rounds earns no points. Speed increases throughout your run. Switch cadence changes timed durations and objective sizes. Select one channel for practice.
